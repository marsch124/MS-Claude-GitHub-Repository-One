How this app works

Browse & search. Scroll the list or type in the search box — it matches the term, full name, explanation and example.

Tap a term. Tapping any card opens a detail view with a deeper explanation, history, examples, scale tables and diagrams where available.

Filter. Tap a category chip (or ★ Favourites). The number shows how many terms it holds.

Favourites. Tap the ☆ on any card to star it.

Edit anything. Every term has ✎ Edit — built-in ones too. The editor opens pre-filled; just change what you like and Save. No duplicating.

See your changes. A built-in term you’ve edited shows its name in an accent colour with an “edited” tag; tap ↺ Reset to restore the original.

Add your own. Tap +. New terms you create are marked with the AMS badge and can be edited (✎) or deleted (×). You can include an emoji, image link and deeper-detail text.

References. Every term has a link — “Learn more” for curated sources or “Look it up” for a web search.

Related terms. A detail view lists related terms — tap one to jump straight to it.

Quiz (🎓). Test yourself with flashcards or multiple-choice. It quizzes whatever filter is active — pick a category or ★ Favourites first to focus.

Backup. Use Export/Import above to back up your terms or move them between devices.
